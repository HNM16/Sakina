Six marks, and what each one does when it moves.

The audit found the current mark fails at launcher size: under 32 px its strokes go sub-pixel and the four tiers merge. These six are simpler by construction, and each is drawn from a different part of the same world.

Every candidate is shown doing the two things a mark in this app has to do: say something arrived, and say something is still happening. Watch the plate on the left, then check the pixel ramp on the right. That ramp is the real test.

Turn it off to see every reduced-motion fallback.

All six at 48 px

A home screen, with the other apps left blank on purpose. This is the size and the company the mark actually keeps, and the only comparison that settles it.

Colour and type are unchanged from docs/BRAND.md: firuza on night, saffron for rare warmth, Noto Sans. Only the form is in question here. Noto stays because the names below need ғ, ӣ, қ, ӯ, ҳ and ҷ, and most display faces do not carry them.

Each animation is built from the three primitives already in docs/MOTION.md — the light pass, the breath, and the turn — so whichever mark wins, the motion language does not have to be rewritten. Nothing here has been seen on a handset.
