The loop

НАВБАТ, БОФТ and ЧАРХ, running at the same time rather than in turn. Showing КОР: the step takes 0.52 s and the light goes round in 2.08 s. Pick a different mix below and this follows it.

The first attempt at this cut the cycle into three windows and faded one behaviour out as the next came in. It did not flow: windowing gives each behaviour a beginning and an end, and however soft the crossfade, the eye still reads three animations taking turns.

So there are no windows here. There is one quantity — the light's position around the weave, which only ever increases — and the other two behaviours are read off it. A bar is saffron when the light is on it. A bar is out of place by how far the light still has to travel to reach it: it reaches ahead along the line it runs on and is home again exactly as the light lands, so the highlight always arrives on something just settled. And the mark turns a quarter for every lap. Nothing starts, nothing stops, and there is one cause instead of three.

Why it read as a game

Not the step. The step was never the problem.

An earlier pass here blamed the instant switch and softened it into a wide travelling light. That was wrong: it made a different animation, and lost the one thing worth keeping. What actually made the original read as a board game is that the step was the only thing happening. Four states and nothing else is a game piece lighting up. The same four states over a weave that is shifting under it and a mark that is turning is a beat over a phrase — same notes, and it stops sounding like a toy.

The hue

Three points around the new accent, all at the same saturation and lightness, so the only thing changing is how green or blue it reads. Click one and the whole page follows.

Firuza now sits at hue 185, moved there from 174 and lifted slightly in saturation and lightness. The old value was a teal leaning green; this one is properly turquoise.

That is truer to where it came from, not a departure from it. The turquoise on Samanid and Timurid tilework runs blue rather than green — the domes at Bukhara and Samarkand are closer to 185 than to 174 — so the move strengthens the reason the colour was chosen rather than trading it away.

What I would pick

КОР for loading. НАФАС for waiting. ТОБ for arrival. ГИРЕҲ for pull-to-refresh. Four states, one rule keeping them apart: the mark is one colour, and saffron only ever means arrived. Nothing in a loop is allowed to spend it — the step inside the loop is the exception the rule was written around, and it is worth being uncomfortable about.

КОР over the other five because nothing in it dominates. ОРОМ is the one I would swap to if this ends up on screen for minutes rather than seconds, and ЗАРБ is the honest fallback if the ripple and the turn turn out to be invisible at 24 px on a real handset — which is exactly the kind of thing that cannot be settled here.

НАФАС stays outside the loop because a pending message is not loading. It is one object in doubt, and it should say so itself rather than summon an indicator to say it on its behalf.

ГУЗАР and МАВҶ are the two alternatives worth keeping on file. ГУЗАР moves light within the bars rather than between them, which is the softer reading if НАВБАТ turns out too punctuated on a real screen; МАВҶ is softer still and has no direction at all. ГИРЕҲ is the most characterful of them and the only one deliberately held back, because a short physical gesture wants a short physical answer rather than a cycle.
